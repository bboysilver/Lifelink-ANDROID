# 2.2.14 (35): morning false-alert investigation

Reported: a tester receives SMS around 07:20-07:30 despite a 12-hour timer and phone use. User confirmed the prefix is `[라이프링크 안부 확인]`: the daily check-in path, not the 12-hour inactivity timer. Previously daily check-in only accepted explicit manual responses. User explicitly authorized normal valid activity to answer daily check-in automatically. Device model, exact use time, app version and device logs are not yet available; the exact reason for the 07:20 delivery time remains unverified.

## Confirmed code defects addressed

- Step-detector registration excluded the accelerometer on most phones. Seated handset movement after unlocking could be missed. Register both; on step-capable phones accept repeated accelerometer motion only while interactive and unlocked. Screen-on and single impact remain insufficient.
- Sensor windows used callback arrival time, losing repeated steps delivered in a burst. Use monotonic sensor event time and retain the actual occurrence time; older events cannot replace newer activity.
- Direct interaction with LifeLink did not update inactivity state. Record actual input, throttled to 30 seconds; merely opening/resuming the activity is not a safety confirmation.
- A service created for SOS could later enter monitoring without registering activity sensors. Ensure listeners before reporting the reused service as running.
- A Room suspension could leave a never-attempted incident after a newer activity reset. Recheck before first dispatch and during recovery; preserve delivery tracking/retries for already-attempted safety incidents.

## Preserved behavior and limits

- Carrier SIM SMS, SOS, guardian selection and actual SMS retry tracking remain in place.
- Daily scheduling remains independent of the inactivity deadline, but valid activity occurring after the daily due time now completes its pending check-in. Old batched events from before the due time cannot answer it. Future due times remain unchanged by early activity. Completed daily check-ins cancel further retries; SMS already handed to the carrier cannot be recalled. Manual confirmations after multi-day delays advance to the next future schedule, not yesterday's schedule.
- No new permission, cloud, location, advertising, accessibility service or usage-history access. Other apps' stationary touches cannot be observed with current permissions. Repeated motion is an activity heuristic, not proof a person is safe.
- Home shows last activity time and reason. Inactivity dispatch log includes its deadline and last activity time/reason locally, without changing SMS data fields.

## Required device regression

1. Record exact SMS prefix, app version, phone model and last-activity display; distinguish inactivity, daily check-in and SOS.
2. On a step-capable phone, unlock once then use LifeLink while seated: confirm timer moves and last activity updates.
3. Use the handset while unlocked and moving repeatedly; confirm activity. Notification-only wake/single vibration must not reset.
4. Overnight with a 12-hour limit: compare last actual recorded activity with alarm time, including around 07:20. Repeat with daily check-in disabled, then enabled separately.
5. Verify SMS delivery for genuine inactivity, force-stop limitations, Doze, reboot, SIM errors and SOS. Emulators/unit tests cannot prove carrier delivery or OEM sensor behavior.
