# LifeLink 2.2.15 (36) release review

## Scope and corrections

The tester confirmed that the morning daily check-in false alert stopped on 2.2.14.
The activity detection policy and carrier-SIM SMS transport remain unchanged.

- Daily notification responses now write directly to the store, without requiring
  health foreground-service startup, activity permission, a working sensor or SIM.
  A response is bound to its exact check-in event, so stale actions cannot confirm a new event.
- Manual SOS now uses a private, user-initiated `shortService` with a five-second
  cancellable countdown. It stops after dispatch; existing workers own callbacks
  and retries. A persisted ordinary worker recovers an interrupted dispatch without
  starting a health service. Recovery timing is not guaranteed by Android.
- A wall-clock change preserves the inactivity event ID, remaining duration and
  actual heartbeat age. Deadline evaluation corrects the clock before deciding to
  send, and the heartbeat interval uses elapsed time rather than wall time.
- Daily response grace is also anchored to elapsed time. After reboot, if elapsed
  time cannot be trusted, the displayed check-in gets a fresh two-hour grace window
  instead of immediately sending a missed-check-in SMS.
- Guardian insertion/deletion is transactional. Duplicate phone numbers and more
  than three guardians are rejected. The last guardian cannot be deleted while
  monitoring, daily check-in or SOS is enabled. Runtime monitoring also checks for
  missing recipients before writing its next heartbeat.
- Retry workers publish final SMS status even if the monitoring service is absent.
  Callback timeout means unconfirmed, not proven non-delivery. Dispatch records for
  unfinished incidents are protected from the 90-day cleanup.
- Room audit state accepts confirmed SMS callbacks after a wall-clock rollback.
  Coroutine cancellation is not swallowed by daily SMS dispatch.
- Listing copy explicitly states that the app is not a medical device and does not
  diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease, and recommends professional medical advice.

## Verification

Regression coverage includes clock jumps/rollback, stable incident identities,
stale daily actions, guardian concurrency, independent SOS without activity
permission on Android 14, SOS cancellation and recovery, SMS terminal notifications
without an active monitoring service, and retention of pending recipients.

Run `gradlew.bat test lint assembleDebug` and the signed GitHub release workflow
before uploading. CI verifies the signature and upload certificate, required SMS
permission, and absence of advertising ID, internet and location permissions.

## Remaining release gates

This is a closed-test release, not evidence of zero future defects or guaranteed
safety. Supported physical phones/SIMs must still complete long-running screen-off,
Doze, reboot, SIM-removal, permission-revocation and actual carrier-SMS tests.
Force-stop, phone power-off and severe vendor background restrictions cannot be
detected remotely by this local-only app. Carrier delivery callbacks are not guaranteed.

Keep Play declarations aligned with this implementation: carrier SMS for physical
safety, activity/health FGS for sensor monitoring, brief SOS shortService, no ads,
and no cloud/location collection. Approval remains a Google Play decision.
Public rollout additionally requires production eligibility and the documented
beta reliability gates. Family subscription and iOS are not enabled by this release.
